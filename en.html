Express Zero (Rychlík Nula) – privacy policy

Effective from 16 November 2026.

Express Zero does not send any personal or game data to the developer or to third parties. The game works offline and contains no ads, analytics, accounts or purchases.

Saved games, your in-game nickname, statistics and settings (sound, controls, read-aloud) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are used to let you continue playing. They are not shared or backed up to the cloud automatically. You can delete them in Android Settings → Apps → Rychlík Nula → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game. This also deletes your saved progress.

If you turn on read-aloud, the game passes the text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. That engine follows its own settings and its provider's terms. The game may use vibration, which requires no data.

Release builds do not write game events to the system log. If you choose to send the developer a screenshot or a bug report, you decide what to share.

Distribution and updates through Google Play are provided by Google under its own terms. This policy covers the game Express Zero (Rychlík Nula) itself.
